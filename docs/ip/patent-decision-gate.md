Before claiming protection, paying fees, re-filing, or licensing the mascots, collect the three application numbers, filing dates, applicant names, correction notices, examination results and current legal status from official records or the applicant's files.

Decision rules:

1. `GRANTED_AND_VALID` only when an official register or certificate and fee/status evidence are available.
2. `PENDING` only when an official application record exists and its status is current.
3. `ABANDONED`, `REJECTED`, `WITHDRAWN` or `EXPIRED` must be recorded exactly; do not replace them with “有专利”.
4. If the record cannot be verified, use `OFFICIAL_RECORD_REQUIRED` and rely on the independently documented original-design/copyright evidence only.

The product must not display a patent wall, patent number, or “专利保护” statement before this gate is closed by documentary evidence and professional review.
