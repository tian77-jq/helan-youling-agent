# Asset Clean Room Policy

- `assets/asset-manifest.json` is the source of truth for production eligibility.
- Internet visibility never implies commercial permission.
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`, `LICENSE_UNKNOWN` and untracked assets cannot enter a buyer production bundle.
- Research screenshots and third-party scenic media belong under `research/`, never under production assets.
- Buyer-provided assets require a written scope, license, territory, term and transfer rule.
